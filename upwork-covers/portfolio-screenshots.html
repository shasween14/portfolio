Screenshot slides — QParking Cloud operator portal

Six real screens from the running app, framed at 1024×768 to match your other portfolio images. Captured by the project's own screenshot harness against a demo instance with demo data, at 1440×900 in the light theme.

Two things deliberately handled:
1. The product wordmark is masked on every slide — the live product brand is your employer's, and these slides go on a public profile. It is replaced with QParking, the name your portfolio already uses for this project. Masks are grey on the two slides whose modal dims the page, so no bright patch shows.
2. Each frame carries a demo data marker, so the figures on screen can never be read as a real client's live revenue. That protects you and it reads as honest to a buyer.
