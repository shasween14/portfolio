About me

Backend first, but I ship the whole thing

Most of my work starts at the database and ends at a receipt printer or a card reader.

I work at Crave Asia as a full-stack developer, leading a few multi-tenant commerce and POS platforms that run in real shops and F&B outlets. Laravel and PHP are my main thing — REST APIs, auth, queues, websockets, PDF/Excel reporting and payment gateways — and I'm just as comfortable on the front-end: HTML/CSS, Blade, Bootstrap and jQuery, and more recently React, TypeScript and Ionic, deployed on Docker with PostgreSQL, Redis and S3-compatible storage. The parts I enjoy most are the awkward ones: offline-first POS, ESC/POS printing, cash drawers, barcode scanning, on-device face recognition.
